# MyLearna Public Positioning V1

## Central promise

Homeschool learning happens everywhere.
MyLearna brings it all together.

## Functional explanation

Plan your week, capture learning as it happens, build meaningful portfolios and
create reports—all in one private family space.

## Product rhythm

Plan → Learn → Capture → Report

- **Plan:** Organise the week and see what is happening today.
- **Learn:** Use the family’s own resources or begin from My Pathways.
- **Capture:** Save photos, notes, worksheets, projects and everyday learning.
- **Report:** Build portfolios and create report PDFs without reconstructing the
  story later.

## Trust reassurance

No password. No credit card. One secure email link opens your private family
space.

## Audience

The intended audience is homeschooling parents and carers who want a calmer way
to connect weekly planning, everyday learning, evidence, portfolios and reports
without scattering the story across separate tools.

## Complement, do not replace

MyLearna complements the books, worksheets, websites, projects, outings and
everyday experiences a family already uses. It organises the learning around
those resources rather than asking families to replace their existing
curriculum or learning style.

## Calls to action

- Primary: **Start free**
- Secondary: **See the Carter Family demo** at `/demo`

## Public-language rules

- Lead with the parent-facing promise, not a feature inventory or technical
  architecture.
- Use “secure email sign-in” or “secure one-time email link.”
- Do not use “OTP” in public customer-facing copy.
- Do not claim “no signup” or imply that an account is not created.
- Do not claim that MyLearna replaces a family’s curriculum or resources.
- Do not advertise unbuilt features, beta status or provisional availability.
- Keep local legal and home-education requirements language factual and
  secondary to the product promise.

## 90-day positioning lock

The central promise remains fixed for 90 days. Creative proof may change, but
the positioning statement should not be rewritten weekly.

## Phase 2 public-page rollout

Extend the same promise and rhythm to selected public support pages after the
homepage and Start Free journey have been reviewed in preview conversion QA.
Potential proof pages include planning, learning evidence, portfolio and
reporting pages. Each rollout should preserve truthful feature boundaries and
avoid changing authenticated product language as part of a marketing-copy
update.

## Measurement expectations

Measure whether the positioning improves understanding and progression through
the public journey, including:

- homepage to Start Free click-through;
- Start Free form completion;
- secure email sign-in link requests;
- check-inbox progression and successful callback completion;
- `/demo` engagement from the homepage;
- mobile versus desktop conversion differences;
- qualitative parent feedback on whether “brings it all together” is clear.

Interpret conversion alongside sign-in delivery and callback reliability so
copy performance is not confused with an authentication service problem.
