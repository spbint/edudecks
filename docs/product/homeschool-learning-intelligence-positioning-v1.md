# MyLearna Homeschool Learning Intelligence Positioning V1

Status: canonical product positioning for preview validation from 12 October
2026. This document does not authorise customer release.

## Category

MyLearna Homeschool is a homeschool learning intelligence product. Planning,
Capture, Portfolio and reports remain important supporting capabilities, but
they are not the primary category claim.

## Core promise

**Know what they know. Know what to do next.**

MyLearna brings trusted assessments and everyday learning evidence together so
families can understand where a child is, see the evidence behind that view,
and know what to teach next — while building the portfolio and reports they
need along the way.

## Competitive contrast

Most homeschool apps help families organise homeschooling. MyLearna helps
families understand the learning.

## Product loop

**Understand → Learn → Capture → Recheck → See change → Report**

- **Understand:** show what current evidence suggests and what remains unknown.
- **Learn:** make the governed next-learning recommendation clear.
- **Capture:** keep authentic work samples, observations and everyday learning.
- **Recheck:** gather new structured evidence when the time is right.
- **See change:** distinguish evidence change from interpretation change without
  manufacturing a growth score.
- **Report:** build Portfolio and reports from evidence already collected.

Planning and calendar capabilities support this loop. They are not removed,
but they do not lead the product promise.

## Evidence is not judgement

Assessment evidence is structured evidence with governed interpretation.
Capture is authentic real-world learning evidence. A photo, parent observation
or work sample does not automatically establish mastery, progression, a
developmental status or a Pathways change. Captured evidence remains distinct
until a separately governed, human-controlled interpretation process uses it.

## Current coverage

Trusted assessment intelligence currently starts with **MyLearna Maths
Starting Point — Number & Operations**. It covers five independent continua and
does not produce one overall Maths score. MyLearna must not imply equivalent
assessment intelligence across all Mathematics or all subjects. Learning
intelligence will be built progressively across the product.

## Australian acquisition wedge

**Understand their learning. Keep the evidence. Be ready to report.**

Australian reporting and compliance are important acquisition benefits. The
core value remains understanding learning and deciding what to do next.
Australian registrations remain paused, with January 2027 retained as the
planned reopening target. Existing production login messaging is outside this
preview-only batch and must not be altered.

## Calls to action while registrations are paused

Public calls to action should lead to the demo, an explanation of how the
product works, or sign-in for an existing family. Account creation must not be
the dominant public action while registration is paused.

## Parent-language rules

Prefer:

- what your child currently appears to know;
- what the evidence suggests;
- what we have seen;
- what remains unknown;
- what to do next;
- current learning view;
- everyday learning evidence;
- since the last check.

Do not require parents to understand internal architecture language such as
canonical constructs, deterministic projections, P-levels, rule versions or
evidence pipelines.

## Feature prioritisation test

> Does this help a family understand what their child knows, understand the
> evidence, decide what to do next, or prove the learning later?

If not, it is secondary.

## Release boundary

This positioning does not enable customer visibility, customer navigation,
persistence, evidence writes, Pathways mutation, hosted acceptance, mobile
acceptance or fresh recheck forms. Product claims must remain inside accepted
capability and preview status until separately authorised.
